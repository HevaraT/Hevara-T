Italy

Why Visit Italy?

Italy is one of Europe's most beautiful and iconic destinations, famous for its romantic cities, stunning coastlines, historic landmarks, world-class food, luxury hotels and unforgettable scenery. Perfect for couples, families, honeymoons, city breaks and multi-centre adventures, Italy offers the perfect mix of culture, relaxation, romance and discovery.

From exploring Rome's ancient history and Venice's canals to relaxing along the Amalfi Coast, tasting wine in Tuscany or island hopping in Sicily, every journey through Italy feels elegant, timeless and unforgettable.

Top Areas To Explore

Rome

Ancient landmarks, beautiful piazzas, fine dining and iconic Italian history

Venice

Romantic canals, gondola rides, luxury hotels and unforgettable city views

Amalfi Coast

Cliffside villages, turquoise waters, beach clubs and breathtaking scenery

Tuscany

Rolling vineyards, countryside escapes, wine tasting and charming towns

Best Time To Visit

Italy is a year-round destination, but the best time to visit is usually during spring and autumn when the weather is pleasant, crowds are lighter and it's ideal for sightseeing, coastal escapes and romantic getaways.

Spring & Early Summer

April – June

Summer Season

July – August

Italy's summer season is lively, sunny and perfect for beach holidays, island escapes, luxury hotels and outdoor dining. Popular areas can be busier, so booking early is recommended.

Autumn Romance

September – October

A beautiful time to visit Italy, with warm weather, vineyard scenery, romantic city breaks and fewer crowds. Perfect for couples, honeymoons, food lovers and luxury multi-centre trips.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa for Italy?

UK passport holders can usually visit Italy for tourism without a visa for up to 90 days in any 180-day period, as Italy is part of the Schengen area. Entry requirements can change, so we always recommend checking the latest official travel advice before travelling.

When is the best time to visit Italy?

The best time to visit Italy is usually between April and June or September and October, when the weather is pleasant and many destinations are less crowded than peak summer.

Is Italy suitable for families and couples?

Yes, Italy is perfect for both families and couples. Families can enjoy beaches, sightseeing, food experiences and resort stays, while couples can enjoy romantic cities, luxury hotels, wine tasting, coastal escapes and beautiful scenery.

Greece is a breathtaking destination where ancient history, beautiful islands, traditional villages and crystal-clear seas come together. From romantic sunsets and whitewashed towns to family-friendly resorts and historic cities, Greece offers an unforgettable holiday for every type of traveller.

Explore the ancient landmarks of Athens, admire the famous caldera views of Santorini, relax on the beautiful beaches of Crete or wander through the medieval streets of Rhodes. Whether you are dreaming of a romantic escape, luxury island retreat, family holiday or cultural adventure, Greece offers the perfect combination of relaxation, history and Mediterranean charm.

Crete

Rhodes

Best Time To Visit

Greece is most popular from spring through to autumn, with warm sunshine, beautiful coastal conditions and a lively holiday atmosphere. FAQs

Do I need a visa for Greece?

British citizens can normally travel to Greece without a visa for tourism for up to 90 days within any 180-day period because Greece is part of the Schengen area. Your passport must meet the relevant issue-date and expiry-date requirements. Always check the latest official travel advice before travelling.

When is the best time to visit Greece?

The best time to visit Greece is generally between April and October. June to August offers the hottest weather and liveliest resorts, while May, September and early October provide warm conditions with fewer crowds.

Which Greek island is best for families?

Crete, Rhodes, Corfu and Kos are popular choices for families because they offer beautiful beaches, family-friendly resorts, activities, waterparks and a wide selection of restaurants.

Is Greece suitable for couples?

Santorini and Mykonos are popular for romantic and luxury escapes, while Crete, Corfu, Rhodes and smaller islands such as Milos also offer beautiful settings for couples.

Is Greece good for island hopping?

Yes. Greece is one of Europe's best island-hopping destinations, with ferry connections and organised routes linking many of the islands. Popular combinations include Santorini and Mykonos, Athens and the Cyclades, or several islands within the Dodecanese.

Is Greece suitable for a luxury holiday?

Absolutely. Greece offers luxury cave suites, private villas, five-star resorts, infinity pools, fine dining, private sailing trips and exclusive beachfront escapes.
