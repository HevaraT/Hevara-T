Italy

Romantic cities, breathtaking coastlines, luxury escapes and unforgettable cultural experiences

Why Visit Italy?

Italy is one of Europe's most beautiful and iconic destinations, famous for its romantic cities, stunning coastlines, historic landmarks, world-class food, luxury hotels and unforgettable scenery. Perfect for couples, families, honeymoons, city breaks and multi-centre adventures, Italy offers the perfect mix of culture, relaxation, romance and discovery.

From exploring Rome's ancient history and Venice's canals to relaxing along the Amalfi Coast, tasting wine in Tuscany or island hopping in Sicily, every journey through Italy feels elegant, timeless and unforgettable.

Top Areas To Explore

Rome

Ancient landmarks, beautiful piazzas, fine dining and iconic Italian history

Venice

Romantic canals, gondola rides, luxury hotels and unforgettable city views

Amalfi Coast

Cliffside villages, turquoise waters, beach clubs and breathtaking scenery

Tuscany

Rolling vineyards, countryside escapes, wine tasting and charming towns

Best Time To Visit

Italy is a year-round destination, but the best time to visit is usually during spring and autumn when the weather is pleasant, crowds are lighter and it's ideal for sightseeing, coastal escapes and romantic getaways.

Spring & Early Summer

April – June

One of the best times to visit Italy, offering warm sunny days, blooming landscapes and perfect conditions for sightseeing, city breaks, countryside escapes and coastal holidays.

Summer Season

July – August

Italy's summer season is lively, sunny and perfect for beach holidays, island escapes, luxury hotels and outdoor dining. Popular areas can be busier, so booking early is recommended.

Autumn Romance

September – October

A beautiful time to visit Italy, with warm weather, vineyard scenery, romantic city breaks and fewer crowds. Perfect for couples, honeymoons, food lovers and luxury multi-centre trips.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa for Italy?

UK passport holders can usually visit Italy for tourism without a visa for up to 90 days in any 180-day period, as Italy is part of the Schengen area. Entry requirements can change, so we always recommend checking the latest official travel advice before travelling.

When is the best time to visit Italy?

The best time to visit Italy is usually between April and June or September and October, when the weather is pleasant and many destinations are less crowded than peak summer.

Is Italy suitable for families and couples?

Yes, Italy is perfect for both families and couples. Families can enjoy beaches, sightseeing, food experiences and resort stays, while couples can enjoy romantic cities, luxury hotels, wine tasting, coastal escapes and beautiful scenery.

Why Visit Dubai?

Dubai is one of the world's most exciting travel destinations, famous for its luxury hotels, futuristic skyline, golden beaches, world-class shopping, fine dining and unforgettable experiences. Perfect for couples, families, luxury escapes, group trips and special celebrations, Dubai offers the perfect mix of sunshine, adventure, relaxation and city glamour.

From relaxing beach resorts and rooftop dining to desert safaris, theme parks, shopping malls and record-breaking attractions, every trip to Dubai feels exciting, stylish and unforgettable.

Top Areas To Explore

Downtown Dubai

Home to the Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall & fountain shows

Dubai Marina

Perfect for waterfront dining, nightlife & luxury stays

Jumeirah Beach

Golden beaches, beach clubs & iconic luxury resorts

Palm Jumeirah

Famous for Atlantis, beach hotels & luxury island escapes

Best Time To Visit

Dubai is a year-round destination, but the best time to visit is usually between November and March when the weather is cooler, sunny and ideal for beach days, sightseeing and outdoor experiences.

Cooler Season

November – March

The most popular time to visit Dubai, offering warm sunny days, comfortable evenings and perfect conditions for exploring the city, relaxing by the pool, enjoying beach clubs and heading out on desert adventures.

Summer Season

June – September

Dubai's summer season is very hot, but can be a great time for luxury hotel deals, indoor attractions, shopping malls, spas, restaurants and family-friendly entertainment.

Luxury Year-Round

Year Round

Dubai is perfect for honeymoons, birthdays, anniversaries, family holidays and luxury escapes throughout the year. With rooftop restaurants, beach clubs, private desert experiences and world-class hotels.
